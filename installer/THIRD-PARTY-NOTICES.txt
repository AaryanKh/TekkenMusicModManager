The program includes a C# port of the Kraken decompressor from ooz
(https://github.com/powzix/ooz), Copyright (C) 2016 Powzix, licensed under the GNU GPL v3 or
later. It is what lets the app read the game's own Oodle-compressed files, so nothing has to be
exported with another tool.

This installer bundles the .NET 8 runtime and WPF (MIT licence, (c) .NET Foundation and
contributors) together with the application. Nothing else is bundled.

The application depends on two tools that are NOT distributed with it and must be installed
separately by the user:

  ffmpeg      https://ffmpeg.org  - decodes your audio files. Builds are licensed under the
              LGPL v2.1+ or GPL v2+/v3 depending on how they were configured. The installer can
              install the "Gyan.FFmpeg" package through winget; that build and its licence come
              from gyan.dev, not from this project.

  UnrealPak   part of Unreal Engine, (c) Epic Games. Subject to the Unreal Engine EULA. The app
              runs the community-distributed build used by the Tekken 8 modding tutorials; obtain
              it and accept Epic's terms yourself.

Optional:

  Rubber Band Library / rubberband CLI - https://breakfastquay.com/rubberband/ - GPL v2+ (or a
              commercial licence). Used only when you point the app at a rubberband executable.

The jukebox slot list (data\jukebox_slots.csv) is derived from the community "Jukebox WEM List"
spreadsheet: IDs and titles only. data\jukebox_text\jukebox_text.json maps those slots to the keys
of the game's title table; the table itself is read from your own install, not shipped.
